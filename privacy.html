Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

LUMOS CREATIVES is a creative digital studio providing Website Development, Video Editing and Content Creation services. We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your personal information responsibly. This policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you visit our website or communicate with us.

What we collect

Our website enquiry form collects your name, email address, selected service, budget and project description. We use this information to understand your requirements and respond to your enquiry.

We use Formspree as our form submission service to process the information you submit through the enquiry form. The submitted information may be delivered to our official business email for communication and follow-up purposes. Formspree may process and store submitted information as necessary to provide its form handling services, in accordance with its own Privacy Policy.

When you contact us by email or other channels, we may also receive contact details and project information that you choose to share.

How we use it

We use the information collected through the enquiry form only to respond to enquiries, understand project requirements, prepare service proposals and communicate with potential clients. We do not use personal information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis or consent where required.

Sharing

LUMOS CREATIVES does not sell or rent personal information to third parties. Information may be processed by essential service providers, such as website hosting, form submission or email providers, where necessary to operate our services. These providers may process information in accordance with their respective privacy policies. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

Cookies and third parties

Our website currently does not intentionally use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics tools.

Our website is hosted on Vercel. Vercel may process basic technical information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to deliver, maintain and secure the website. This information may be processed or stored on servers outside India, depending on Vercel's infrastructure. Vercel handles it under its own Privacy Policy.

Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests these fonts, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address. Google operates under its own privacy policy.

Security and retention

We take reasonable measures to protect information shared with us against unauthorized access, misuse, loss or disclosure. Personal information is kept only for as long as necessary to fulfil its purpose or meet legal requirements, and we take reasonable steps to delete it when it is no longer required.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and the rules in force in India, you may have rights relating to your personal data, including requesting access, correction and erasure, and raising privacy-related grievances, subject to applicable legal requirements. You may contact us to exercise these rights or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Contact and grievances

For questions, privacy concerns, data requests or grievances about this policy, email us at lumoscreatives1726@gmail.com. We will review and respond within a reasonable period, in accordance with applicable requirements.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, website practices or legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date. Please review it from time to time.
